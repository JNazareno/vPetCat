Adopt a cat

Pick a coat and a name. Every cat has its own personality and its own favourite (and least favourite) places to be petted.

How to care for your cat

  • Look around: drag empty space to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Double-click the cat to follow it.
  • Pet: with ✋, stroke the cat. Each cat has favourite spots (chin, cheeks...). Find them in the Cat tab. Belly rubs are often a trap.
  • Read the signals: a lashing tail and flattened ears mean stop, or you'll get swatted.
  • Pick up: press and hold on the cat, then carry it. Drop it in the bed or on the sofa.
  • Play: flick toys across the floor, use the 🔴 laser or the 🪶 feather wand. Catches earn 🪙.
  • Care: click the bowls to refill them and the litter box to scoop it. A full box means accidents.
  • Time: one real second is one game minute at 1×. The cat eats, naps in sunbeams, watches birds and lives its life on its own.
  • Growing up: time flies for cats. Your kitten arrives at 8 weeks old, and each game day is about 2 months of its life: a junior by day 3, an adult by day 6.

Settings
